Our publishing tool

To the Last Second has a small publishing tool of its own. It runs on the producer's computer and uploads the channel's own videos, with their titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails, to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, where the producer reviews and publishes them. It isn't offered to anyone else, and it collects no data about viewers.
