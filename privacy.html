TO THE LAST SECOND

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Who we are

To the Last Second (حتى آخر ثانية) is a YouTube channel of animated true stories, with accounts on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. It is run by Yousef Hamza. Contact: yousefhamza526@gmail.com.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics or advertising, has no forms, and loads nothing from other sites: its fonts and images come from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security, as its privacy statement describes.

Our publishing tool

The channel has a small publishing tool of its own (“the app”). It runs on the producer's computer and is used only by the channel's producer, to upload the channel's own videos, titles, descriptions, captions and thumbnails to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, as private videos or drafts that the producer then reviews and publishes. It is not offered to anyone else.

  • What it accesses: only the channel's own accounts, through the permissions the producer grants when signing in, and only to upload the channel's content and to read what it needs to confirm an upload, such as the new video's ID and processing status. It does not read anyone else's data, messages or comments.
  • What it stores, and where: the sign-in tokens and a log of what was uploaded where, kept only on the producer's computer. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • What it shares: nothing. Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Viewers and other users: the app collects no data about viewers or any other user.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies to the information YouTube handles. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time on Google's security settings page.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and TikTok

The app uses Meta's APIs to post to the channel's own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, and TikTok's Content Posting API to send videos to the channel's own TikTok accounts. Their policies apply to the information they handle: the Meta Privacy Policy and the TikTok Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked in Facebook's settings under Business integrations, and in TikTok's settings under Security, Manage app permissions.

Deleting data

The app holds only the channel's own sign-in tokens and upload log, so it holds nothing about anyone else. To remove its access, revoke it in the account settings above. To have the tokens and the log deleted, email yousefhamza526@gmail.com; they are deleted within 30 days.

Children

The channel is made for teens and adults, not for children, and the app is not used by children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and its date changed.
